Capabilities

Coverage Without Audit History

New languages have little audit history to learn from. Through cross-language learning, WizAud brings experience from mature ecosystems to code that has never had it.

Targeted Analysis

Reading an entire codebase with a language model is slow and expensive. WizAud first identifies the regions most likely to be exploitable, then directs deep analysis only there, so compute goes where the risk is.

Confidence-Ranked Leads

Every lead is ranked by confidence and labelled with where the knowledge behind it came from. Your team sees the strongest signals first and decides how deep to go, without wading through noise.
